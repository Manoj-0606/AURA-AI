Strength training builds muscle and increases metabolism.

Progressive overload means gradually increasing weight,
reps, or intensity over time.

A balanced workout routine should include:

- Push exercises
- Pull exercises
- Leg training
- Core work
- Mobility training

For fat loss:

- Maintain calorie deficit
- Lift weights 3-5 times weekly
- Do cardio 2-4 times weekly
- Sleep 7-9 hours
